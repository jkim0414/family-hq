🏡 Family HQ — Privacy

Last updated September 28, 2026

Family HQ is a private, single-household application used only by the members of the family. It is not offered to the public and has no other users. This policy describes what Family HQ collects, how it is used, and who processes it.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar (calendar.events, via Google sign-in): to create, update, and read events on the family's shared calendar, and to read household members' work calendars that they have chosen to share, as read-only planning context.
  • Gmail (read-only, via Gmail app passwords): to find messages about the household (school, activities, appointments, travel) in the household members' own inboxes, and to search them when asked. The app never sends, modifies, labels, or deletes email in these inboxes.
  • Logins: a dedicated 1Password vault the household controls, used to sign in to websites for tasks a household member requests. Passwords are filled in directly and are never shown to the AI model.
  • Phone numbers and text messages: the mobile numbers of household members who opt in to texts, their opt-in status, and the content of texts exchanged with Kimi.

How data is used and stored

Data is used solely to run the family's own calendar, to-do list, and assistant. Messages identified as household-related are stored with what was filed from them. Other messages are screened using only the sender, subject, and a short excerpt, and are not stored. Everything is kept in the family's private database and hosting accounts.

Phone numbers and text messages are used only to deliver Kimi's messages and replies to household members who opted in, and to record opt-in and opt-out requests.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. No data is sold or used for advertising. Data is processed only by the service providers that run Family HQ, for that purpose: Anthropic (AI processing, which under its commercial terms is not used to train models), Vercel (hosting), Upstash (database), Twilio (text messages), Browserbase (web browser for requested tasks), Google (calendar and email), and 1Password (logins).

Limited Use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

Text STOP to +1 (555) 010-0100 at any time to stop text messages. A household member can disconnect Gmail, work calendars, or other connections from the Kimi tab in the app, or remove Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored credential.
