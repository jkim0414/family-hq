The text-message program

Kimi, the Family HQ assistant, texts household members about the family's schedule and logistics: replies to their questions, confirmations of events and to-dos she files, time-sensitive alerts, requests for approval before she acts, and the results of tasks she completes. The program is limited to Family HQ household members who opt in.

How to join: text START to +1 (555) 010-0100, then reply Y to the welcome message to confirm. See the sign-up page for the full exchange.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply HELP for help. Text HELP (or INFO) to +1 (555) 010-0100, or email alex@example.com.

Reply STOP to opt out. Text STOP (or UNSUBSCRIBE, END, QUIT, CANCEL) to +1 (555) 010-0100 at any time. You'll receive one confirmation and no further messages. Text START to rejoin.
